Our Scientific Methodology

HealthsenseAI leverages advanced clinical classification models and wellness analytics to support daily health assessments.

Dual-Layer Health Assistance

Layer 1: Symptom Classification

Uses a trained Support Vector Classifier (SVC) tuned to identify 41 distinct pathologies from a network of 132 physiological indicator parameters. Our model assesses symptom co-occurrence weights to verify match reliability.

Layer 2: Wellness Tracker

Monitors metabolic calorie balances and macronutrient distributions (protein, carbohydrates, fats) based on dietary logs and workout expenditures. Ensures patients meet physical fitness and nutritional guidelines.

Clinical Triage Disclaimer

HealthsenseAI is developed as a pre-clinical awareness tool. It is engineered to support patient awareness and preliminary clinical classification. It is not an active substitute for primary care medical diagnosis, lab tests, pathology reviews, or direct consultations with licensed practitioners.
